Quick Answers

Frequently asked

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, but we strongly recommend booking — especially on weekends. Walk-in tables are first-come, first-served and wait times can be 30–45 minutes during peak hours.

Do you cater for private events?

Absolutely. We host birthday parties, corporate lunches, and family gatherings. Call us at +91 94310 00000 or use the contact form above to discuss your event.

Is parking available?

Yes, City Center Mall has a multi-level car park. We validate the first two hours of parking for all dining guests — just show your bill at the exit.

Are all dishes authentically from Jharkhand?

Every recipe on our menu traces back to the Lakra family kitchen or to Santhali, Munda, and Oraon tribal traditions. We never use pre-packaged spice mixes or shorten cooking times.
